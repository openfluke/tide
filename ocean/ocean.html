Each card is a live tide. Ocean polls /api/board — it does not retrain. Remote Pis POST /api/register then wait for Start all. Score = T × Availability% × SoftAcc% / 10,000. LPD gold is ≥80% of that Score champ at ≤20% of its RAM — that is the recipe, not raw Score/MiB (binary trap). A worker can pin -modes sgd,step_sgd so three Zeros split the train-mode axis.
