Lucy density — synthetic organism

The thing being measured is a net that runs and trains at the same time in a small box. Pure Acc is not enough: SGD can look accurate while it blocks serve and burns RAM. Score = Thru × Avail × Acc / 10,000 (Welvet). Q is Acc/Thru/Avail vs learner peaks. LPD = Q × shrink vs Acc-champ RAM; 0 unless Acc keep ≥70%. Gold = all three ≥80% at ≤20% Acc-champ RAM. Gold-std = Acc ≥80% plus Thru or Avail, then smallest then fastest. Honesty / test48 is where FastProxy and MeshTweenSplitSparse show up as the paths that keep the live loop.

gold trifecta, small RAM near 2+ pillars, ≤50% RAM keep 2+ pillars, fat acc-only trap tiny RAM, Acc<70%
