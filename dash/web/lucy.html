Lucy density — synthetic organism

The thing being measured is a net that runs and trains at the same time in a small box. Pure Hard Acc is not enough: SGD can look accurate while it blocks serve and burns RAM. Hard Acc = argmax. Acc keep % = Hard Acc ÷ Acc-champ Acc (not SoftAcc). Score = Thru × Avail × Hard Acc / 10,000. Q = geomean Acc-keep / Thru-keep / Avail-keep vs learner peaks. LPD = Q × shrink vs Acc-champ RAM; 0 unless Acc keep ≥70%. Gold = all three pillars ≥80% at ≤20% Acc-champ RAM. Gold-std = Acc keep ≥80% plus Thru or Avail, then smallest then fastest. Lean (≥95%) = Acc keep ≥95% of Acc champ, then smallest RAM / fastest Thru / best Avail — the footprint win without collapsing Acc.
